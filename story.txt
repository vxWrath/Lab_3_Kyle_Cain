The radio chatter in Marcus’s earpiece buzzed with static as he leaned over the ledge of the sky-rise. Twelve stories below, a fractured water main had turned the downtown intersection into a churning whirlpool. A stranded school bus tilted dangerously on the buckling asphalt, its rear tires slipping into the rushing current.

Marcus closed his eyes and breathed in, reaching for the spark beneath his ribs.

He didn't wear armor or fly through the clouds. His power was raw, kinetic resonance—the ability to manipulate vibration and momentum. To the city, he was **Vanguard**, a blur of copper light in the dark. To himself, he was just a man trying to balance a heavy debt to the world.

He stepped off the ledge.

Wind roared in his ears as gravity pulled him down. Right before impact, Marcus released a localized pulse beneath his boots, absorbing his momentum to land silently on the wet pavement.

The bus gave a sickening screech as the street dissolved. Inside, terrified faces pressed against the glass.

"Hold on!" Marcus shouted, lunging beneath the front bumper and pressing his palms flat against the steel. Veins of glowing orange light surged across his arms as he converted the rushing water's energy into pure, upward lift, pulling the thirty-ton bus backward out of the sinkhole inch by agonizing inch.

The metal groaned, then settled onto stable pavement. Marcus collapsed to his knees, gasping as his glowing skin faded.

He looked up, catching the eye of a frightened child in the front seat. The boy didn't look relieved—he pointed toward the bus driver in terror. Marcus turned just in time to see the driver smile, pull a glowing detonator from his jacket, and press it.

The sinkhole hadn't been an accident. The bus was a trap.

Instead of an explosion, a high-frequency shockwave burst from beneath the vehicle, locking Marcus’s kinetic powers in a feedback loop and paralyzing his heart. As he fell sideways into the mud, the "scared" children inside unbuckled their seatbelts in eerie unison, their eyes glowing faint neon purple. The city’s greatest hero hadn't just saved a school bus—he had just delivered a sleeper cell of weaponized meta-humans straight into the heart of the city.
